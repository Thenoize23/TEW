How it works

Three steps, about ten seconds.

No plugin to install, no upload bar, no waiting for an email. Your browser already has an audio engine; Mix Check just asks it the right questions.

01

Drop a bounce

A WAV, MP3, FLAC or AIFF of your mix or your master. It is decoded and measured right here, in this tab.

02

Measured against your subgenre

A techno master and a boom bap mix are not judged by the same numbers. Each of the 19 subgenres carries its own loudness, dynamics and low-end ranges.

03

The step that fixes it

The worst problem points at a real step in your subgenre's pack: a mono problem lands on the phone and car check, a crushed master on mix bus glue.

What it reads

Six readings. The ones that decide how a record sounds next to other records.

Loudness

LUFS

Integrated loudness to ITU-R BS.1770: K-weighted, with both gates. The number streaming platforms turn your track up or down to.

Dynamics

PLR

Peak to loudness ratio: how much punch survived the limiter. Under your subgenre's range, the drums have stopped hitting.

Peak

dBFS

Checked against the right ceiling for the file: -3 dB for a mix that still has to be mastered, -0.3 dB for a master.

Mono compatibility

L/R

Left/right correlation, and how much of the low end sits in the sides below 120 Hz. Phones, clubs and Bluetooth speakers are mono down there.

Spectral balance

6 BANDS

Sub to air, each band in dB against the 400 Hz – 2 kHz body of the track, so the shape compares across files at any level.

Low-end weight

SUB+BASS

How far the sub and bass sit above the body, against the range your subgenre carries. Thin or muddy, it says which.

Private by construction

Your stems stay yours.

Producers do not send unreleased music to strangers, and they are right not to. So Mix Check does not ask you to: there is no upload step, because the whole analysis runs in the browser you are reading this in.

FAQ

Questions

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is decoded and measured by your browser, inside this tab. It never leaves your machine, and closing the tab is the end of it.

Is Mix Check really free?

Yes: no account, no card and no limit on files. The report names the step that fixes your worst problem; the full step lives in the app, which also starts free.

How accurate are the numbers?

Loudness follows ITU-R BS.1770, the standard streaming platforms use, and the measurements are checked against test signals. The targets are typical ranges for each subgenre, not rules: a record that breaks them on purpose is still a record.

Should I check the mix or the master?

Either, and tell it which. A mix is judged on headroom and dynamics it should leave for mastering; a master is judged on where your subgenre sits on a playlist.

Does the workpad replace my DAW?

No. It sits next to it. You work in your DAW; the workpad tells you what step you're on and how far the track actually is.

Who is it for?

Solo producers and mix or mastering engineers working without a team: anyone who has to be their own second opinion.
